# RD FITZ style guide

## Visual direction
The product uses the Neutral Focus style.
Background color is off white `#F5F5F5`.
Accent color is emerald `#00C853`.
UI relies on flat silhouette illustration assets and bold geometric controls.

## Components
Segmented controls are used for high level navigation.
Filter chips are used for equipment and focus area filtering.
Streak counter appears on planner and progress screens.
Cards use sharp hierarchy with title, supporting meta row, and one clear call to action.

## Motion and feedback
Use short haptic patterns for reminder, completion, and milestone events.
Use short achievement sounds with low latency playback.
Avoid decorative motion and avoid visual clutter.

## Copy rules
Use short directive language.
Do not include emojis.
Do not include dashed formatting in user facing assistant copy.
